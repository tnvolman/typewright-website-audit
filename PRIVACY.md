# Privacy

Typewright Website Audit is published by Michael McClanahan, an individual doing business as Typewright Studio.

The website address you submit is sent to typewrightstudio.com, which fetches that public page. The speed check sends that address to Google PageSpeed. Neither the URL nor the results are stored. Rate limiting keeps a salted hash of the caller's IP in memory for at most 24 hours.

Audit data is not sold and is never used to train AI models.
